# Download Theme Files - ThemeForest

When you purchase a theme from ThemeForest, you can download the theme files via the ‘Downloads’ tab in your ThemeForest account. To get both the theme files and supporting documentation, select the ‘All Files & Documentation’ option during download.

Step 1 – Log in to your _**ThemeForest account**_ and navigate to the _**‘Downloads’ tab.**_

Step 2 – Locate your theme purchase and click the _**Download button**._ Choose _**‘All Files & Documentation’**_ to download both the theme files and the supporting documents.
